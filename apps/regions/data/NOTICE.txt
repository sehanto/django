wilayah.csv.gz: Indonesian administrative regions and postcodes
================================================================

code,name,postal_code for every province (38), kabupaten/kota (514), kecamatan (7,285)
and kelurahan/desa (83,762), with the Kemendagri codes of Kepmendagri No. 300.2.2-2138
Tahun 2025. Postcodes are given for kelurahan/desa only.

Built from:
- https://github.com/cahyadsn/wilayah (db/wilayah.sql, last edit 2026-02-13)
- https://github.com/cahyadsn/wilayah_kodepos (db/wilayah_kodepos.sql)
