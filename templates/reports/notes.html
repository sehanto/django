2. {% translate "Significant accounting policies" %}

  1. {% translate "Statement of compliance" %}. {% translate "These financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the Financial Accounting Standards for Micro, Small and Medium Entities (SAK EMKM)." %}
  2. {% translate "Basis of preparation" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}The financial statements are prepared on the accrual basis and at historical cost, on the assumption that the business is a going concern. The business is accounted for separately from its owners: their personal transactions are not recorded.{% endblocktranslate %}
  3. {% translate "Presentation currency" %}. {% translate "The financial statements are presented in Rupiah, the currency of the business's main economic environment." %}
  4. {% translate "Revenue and expenses" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Revenue from the sale of goods and services is recognised when the invoice is issued, that is when the goods have been delivered or the services rendered, at the gross amount excluding PPN. Payments received before the goods or services are delivered are a liability (revenue received in advance) until then. Revenue and costs of construction contracts are recognised as billed. Interest and dividends, including distributions from joint ventures, are recognised when received; rent and royalties received in advance are recognised straight-line over the contract. Grants are income when received, unless they are in substance a contribution of capital by the owner. Expenses are recognised when they are incurred, regardless of when they are paid: rent and other expenses paid in advance are presented as prepaid expenses and expensed straight-line over the period they cover. Sales and purchase returns are recorded in the period of the return.{% endblocktranslate %}
  5. {% translate "Receivables" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Trade receivables are stated at the amount invoiced, less what has been received. As SAK EMKM provides (8.10), no allowance for doubtful debts is made; a receivable that is unlikely to be collected is written off and the loss is recognised as an expense (8.11).{% endblocktranslate %}
  6. {% translate "Inventory" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed with formula=org.get_inventory_cost_formula_display %}Inventory is measured at cost: the purchase price and the other costs of bringing it to its present location and condition. The cost of inventory sold is determined using the {{ formula }} formula. Stock is recorded perpetually: purchases are added to inventory, and the cost of goods sold is recognised when each sale is invoiced. Goods bought without stock records are expensed when purchased. Inventory that can no longer be sold for its cost is written down to its net realisable value, and the loss is recognised in the period.{% endblocktranslate %}
  7. {% translate "Fixed assets" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed with method=org.get_depreciation_method_display %}Fixed assets are measured at cost less accumulated depreciation; no impairment is recognised. Land is not depreciated; other fixed assets are depreciated monthly over their useful lives using the {{ method }} method, without a residual value, from the month they are acquired and ready for use. Repairs and maintenance are expensed. Changes in useful life or depreciation method are changes in estimate, applied prospectively. When an asset is sold, the difference between the proceeds and its book value is other income or expense; when it is given away or scrapped, its book value is an other expense.{% endblocktranslate %}
  8. {% translate "Intangible assets" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Intangible assets acquired separately (e.g. software, licences, franchises) are measured at cost less accumulated amortisation, amortised over their limited useful lives without a residual value; no impairment is recognised. Intangible items generated internally, such as research, brands, start-up costs, training and advertising, are expensed.{% endblocktranslate %}

  9. {% translate "Investments in joint ventures" %}. {% translate "Investments in joint ventures are measured at cost and presented as assets; no impairment is recognised." %}
    10. {% endif %}
  10. {% translate "Employee benefits" %}. {% translate "Salaries, allowances and the employer's BPJS contributions are expenses of the month in which the employees earn them, at the undiscounted amount payable; severance is an expense when the termination is decided. PPh 21 and PPh 26 withheld and BPJS contributions not yet paid are presented as liabilities." %}
  11. {% translate "Liabilities" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Liabilities are stated at the amount needed to settle them; loans at the amount borrowed less repayments. Borrowing costs and the transaction costs of loans are expensed when incurred. Provisions and contingent liabilities are not recognised; material ones are disclosed under additional information.{% endblocktranslate %}
  12. {% translate "Foreign currency" %}. {% translate "Transactions in a foreign currency are recorded in Rupiah at the spot rate on the date of the transaction." %}
  13. {% translate "Equity" %}. {% if org.is_limited_company %}{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Equity consists of paid-up share capital, additional paid-in capital (what shareholders paid above the shares' nominal value) and retained earnings. Dividends reduce retained earnings when the shareholders declare them.{% endblocktranslate %} {% elif org.is_cooperative %}{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Equity consists of the members' principal and compulsory savings (simpanan pokok and simpanan wajib), reserves and the retained surplus (sisa hasil usaha). Surplus distributed to members reduces equity when the members' meeting decides it.{% endblocktranslate %} {% elif org.is_partnership %}{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Equity consists of the partners' capital and retained earnings. Amounts the partners withdraw for their own use (prive) reduce equity.{% endblocktranslate %} {% else %}{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Equity consists of the owner's capital and retained earnings. Amounts the owner withdraws for personal use (prive) reduce equity.{% endblocktranslate %}{% endif %}
  14. {% translate "Accounting policies, estimates and errors" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}The policies above are applied consistently. Changes in estimates are applied prospectively; errors of prior periods are corrected retrospectively, restating the comparative figures.{% endblocktranslate %}
  15. {% translate "Income tax" %}. {% if org.uses_pph_final %}{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Income tax is the final income tax for MSMEs under PP 55/2022, 0.5% of gross turnover. It is recognised as an expense in the month the turnover arises, and any amount not yet paid is presented as a liability. Because the tax is final, no deferred tax is recognised.{% endblocktranslate %} {% else %}{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Income tax expense is the tax payable for the period under the tax regulations. As SAK EMKM provides, no deferred tax is recognised.{% endblocktranslate %}{% endif %} {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Income tax that customers withhold from their payments is presented as prepaid tax (PPh 22 and 23) until it is credited, or settles part of the month's final income tax liability (PPh Final). Income tax withheld from payments to suppliers (PPh 21, 23, 26 and 4(2)) is a liability until it is deposited. Final income tax the business deposits itself on its own income (e.g. on rent) is an expense of the month of that income. PPh 22 paid on imports is prepaid tax.{% endblocktranslate %}
    16. {% if org.is_pkp %}
  16. {% translate "Value added tax (PPN)" %}. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}As a VAT-registered business (PKP), PPN charged to customers and PPN paid to suppliers is not revenue or expense. The balances are presented as a liability (output VAT) and an asset (input VAT). PPN that customers appointed as VAT collectors withhold and deposit settles the output VAT; PPN on deliveries under a facility (not collected or exempt) is not charged. PPnBM on luxury goods sold is a liability; PPnBM paid on purchases is part of their cost. PPN paid at customs on imports is input VAT; exports bear PPN at 0%.{% endblocktranslate %}
    17. {% endif %}
